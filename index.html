Jane Artist

New York, United States

Jane Artist is known for her vibrant abstract paintings that explore the relationship between color and emotion. With over 15 years of experience, her work has been exhibited in galleries across North America and Europe.

Her latest collection "Emotional Landscapes" delves into the visual representation of complex human emotions through bold brushstrokes and vivid color palettes.
